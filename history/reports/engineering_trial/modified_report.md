- F1：An outer barrel has a cylindrical connecting end and a conical tip end.

- F2：An ink reservoir tube with a ballpoint moves axially when a cap is rotated, extending or retracting the writing tip.

- F3：An externally threaded hollow rod engages a tapped hole of an intermediate tube to convert rotation into limited axial movement.

- F4：A compression spring biases a non-rotatable annular engaging member into contact with a rotatable annular engaging member.

- F5：The fixed and rotatable engaging members have annular crowns of teeth.

- F6：The annular tooth crowns of the fixed and rotatable engaging members are made of polyoxymethylene (POM) polymer instead of metal and still produce audible clicking when engaging and disengaging.
